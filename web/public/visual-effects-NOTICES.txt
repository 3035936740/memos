Third-party visual assets used by Memos

Cyotek SkylineGenerator - skyline window layout
Copyright (c) 2015 Cyotek Ltd. MIT License.
Source: https://github.com/cyotek/SkylineGenerator/blob/master/src/SimpleSkylineGenerator.cs
Full license: docs/licenses/skyline-generator-MIT.txt
Memos adapts the building-local window layout and lighting density into static SVG paths,
with new layered architecture, four additional distant city planes, a promenade,
foreground palms and theme-aware sunset colours. The scene module loads only for
the vaporwave style; the geometry remains static.
Generated at development time by web/scripts/generate-vaporwave-skyline.mjs;
no runtime generator, scrolling animation, or external image request is used.

Twemoji - Candy
Copyright 2019 Twitter, Inc and other contributors.
Source: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/blob/master/assets/svg/1f36c.svg
Graphics licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Memos preserves the original SVG geometry, recolors it into five palettes and
rasterizes each variant once for animated canvas particles.

management and transparent compositing over its interactive article pages.
